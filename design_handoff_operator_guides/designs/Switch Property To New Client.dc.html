Switch a property to a new client

Hand a property from an old client to a new one, cleanly and without missed leads.

5 steps 2 systems
{{ doneCount }} of 5 steps checked off

Why this matters

The old client is done and a new one is taking over this property. Leads must stop going to the old client and start going to the new one, and the billing has to change hands cleanly. Get it half-right and either the old client keeps getting leads they no longer pay for, or the new client gets nothing.

Two ways a half-done switch fails
✕ Workflow still points at the old client → they keep getting leads they no longer pay for
✕ Assignment swapped but notifications untouched → the new client gets nothing
